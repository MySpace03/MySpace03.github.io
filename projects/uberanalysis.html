THE QUESTION

Where does supply fail to meet demand?

Using statistical analysis and exploratory data analysis across more than 500,000 Uber trip records, I investigated the patterns behind peak-hour cancellations. The analysis identified airport-to-city supply shortfalls as the primary driver.

I created more than 10 Power BI and Tableau dashboards to communicate the findings, then proposed three supply-side interventions projected to reduce cancellations by 20% and improve driver utilization.
